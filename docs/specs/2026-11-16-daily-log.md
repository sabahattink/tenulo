# Tenulo 1.1: one daily log (food, water, measurements, exercise), Turkish and English

Status: accepted, in progress on `feat/daily-log`.

## Why

People on a GLP-1 medication log their shots in Tenulo, then go to other apps for what they eat,
how much water they drink, their tape-measure numbers and their exercise. The owner's direction:
one app for all of it, as detailed as a food diary where you pick a meal, search a food ("yumurta"),
choose the portion and count, and the energy is added to the day. First market: Türkiye, so the app
ships in Turkish and English.

## Goals

- Food diary: four meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks) per day; search a bundled food list in
  Turkish or English; pick a portion (piece, slice, bowl, glass, tablespoon, gram…) and an amount;
  energy (kcal) and protein shown, carbohydrate, fat and fibre kept. Recent foods first. The user
  can add their own foods.
- Water: one tap adds a glass (size set by the user); daily total against the user's own goal.
- Body measurements: waist, hip, chest, neck, upper arm, thigh; history and a trend per site.
- Exercise: type, duration, intensity, optional kcal the user knows (from a watch, say); an
  estimate is shown when they do not enter one.
- Daily goals the user sets themselves: energy, protein, water. Tenulo never computes or suggests
  a target (same rule as the dose plan).
- A Today screen that shows the whole day at a glance and starts every log in one tap.
- Turkish and English everywhere: screens, PDF report, reminders, store listing.
- New illustrations in the existing paper-cut style (made in Canva).

## Non-goals (this release)

- Barcode scanning or online food search: the camera is a blocked permission and the app makes no
  network calls. The food list is bundled.
- Reading steps, exercise, nutrition or hydration from Health Connect. AGENTS.md: any record type
  beyond Weight needs the privacy policy updated first. Health Connect stays Weight, read-only.
- Meal plans, recipes, coaching, streaks, social features.

## Anti-goals

- Any number that reads as advice: no recommended calories, protein or water, no "you should".
- Any drug brand name, and no food brand names either (generic foods only).
- Data leaving the phone other than through the user's own export.

## Constraints (from AGENTS.md, unchanged)

- No floats in the database: kcal integer, nutrients integer milligrams, water integer ml, lengths
  integer millimetres, amounts integer thousandths (`quantityMilli`, 1.5 pieces = 1500).
- Dates are `LocalDate`; instants are UTC epoch ms; day arithmetic through `src/domain/date.ts`.
- Schema changes through `src/db/schema.ts` + `pnpm db:generate`; never edit a shipped migration.
  Date-format checks on new tables are table CHECKs (new tables are not referenced by old ones).
- "Delete all data" removes every row in every table (`ALL_TABLES_DELETE_ORDER`), including the new
  ones; the backup file carries every table (format version 2; version 1 files stay readable).
- `src/domain/` stays pure TypeScript with unit tests for every function.

## Data model (new)

`settings` gains:

| column | type | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `language` | text `system`/`tr`/`en`, default `system` | UI language; `system` follows the phone |
| `energy_goal_kcal` | int null, 500–6000 | the user's own daily energy goal |
| `protein_goal_g` | int null, 10–400 | the user's own daily protein goal |
| `water_goal_ml` | int null, 250–10000 | the user's own daily water goal |
| `water_serving_ml` | int, default 250, 50–2000 | what one tap of "+ glass" adds |

New tables (ULID ids, `created_at`/`updated_at` like the others):

- `food_entry`: `local_date`, `logged_at`, `meal` (`breakfast`/`lunch`/`dinner`/`snack`),
  `catalog_food_id` text null (id in the bundled list), `custom_food_id` text null → `custom_food`
  (on delete set null), `name` (snapshot, 1–80), `portion_label` (snapshot, 1–40),
  `quantity_milli` (1–100000), `grams` int null, `energy_kcal` (0–10000), `protein_mg`,
  `carb_mg`, `fat_mg`, `fiber_mg` (int null, 0–2000000). Nutrients are a snapshot taken when the
  food is logged, so history never changes when the food list is regenerated. Exactly one of the two
  food ids is set, or neither once a custom food was deleted.
- `custom_food`: `name` (1–60), `serving_label` (1–40), `serving_grams` int null, `energy_kcal`
  per serving (0–5000), `protein_mg`, `carb_mg`, `fat_mg`, `fiber_mg` (int null).
- `water_entry`: `local_date`, `logged_at`, `amount_ml` (1–5000).
- `measurement_entry`: `local_date`, `measured_at`, `site` (`waist`/`hip`/`chest`/`neck`/`arm`/
  `thigh`), `length_mm` (100–3000), `note`. Shown in cm, or in inches when the weight unit is lb.
- `exercise_entry`: `local_date`, `logged_at`, `kind` (`walking`/`running`/`cycling`/`swimming`/
  `strength`/`yoga`/`sports`/`other`), `duration_min` (1–1440), `intensity`
  (`light`/`moderate`/`vigorous`), `energy_kcal` int null (only what the user entered), `note`.

## Food list (bundled)

- Source: USDA FoodData Central "SR Legacy" (public domain, CC0) for ingredients and simple foods;
  Turkish dishes computed from documented standard recipes over those ingredients. Every number is
  traceable: the generator script and its inputs are in the repo, the output is a generated
  TypeScript module. No value is typed in from memory.
- Each food: stable id, Turkish and English names (+ search aliases), category, energy and
  nutrients per 100 g (integers), portions (`{id, label tr/en, grams}`), default portion first.
  Liquids use 1 g = 1 ml.
- Search folds case and Turkish letters (`kofte` finds "köfte", `İ/ı` handled), matches word
  starts, ranks recent foods first.

## Screens

Tabs: **Today**, **Food**, **Progress**, **History**, **Settings**.

- Today: date and greeting; a day card with three rings (energy, protein, water: value and the
  user's goal, or just the value with a "set a goal" link); quick actions (food, water, weight,
  measurement, exercise, side effect); the weight trend card; the injection card; today's exercise.
- Food: a day pager (previous/next day, "today"); per meal a section with its entries and an "Add"
  button; day totals at the top. Add flow: search screen (recents, then results) → portion sheet
  (portion chips, amount stepper, live kcal/protein) → saved to that meal. "My foods" for custom
  foods (create, edit, delete).
- Progress: weight trend chart (moved here in full; Today keeps the summary), measurements per
  site with a small chart and latest change, weekly averages of energy, protein and water.
- History: the existing timeline plus food (one line per meal), water (one line per day), measurements
  and exercise, with filters.
- Settings: language, goals (energy, protein, water, glass size), "My foods", the existing items.

## Pro

All logging stays free. Pro keeps the PDF doctor report (which gains nutrition, water, measurement
and exercise summaries) and Health Connect weight import.

## Languages

- No i18n dependency: typed dictionaries (`en`, `tr`; the Turkish one must satisfy the same type),
  numbers and dates through `Intl` with the active locale (Turkish decimal comma).
- `system` picks Turkish when the phone's language is Turkish, otherwise English.
